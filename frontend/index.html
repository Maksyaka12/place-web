Place: all in one place

Place is onchain infrastructure on Base: anyone creates a reward campaign for the holders of any ERC-20 token or NFT collection, and holders see every live and upcoming reward and claim them all in one place, by themselves or through an AI agent.

  • Explore: live and upcoming campaigns across all projects.
  • My rewards: everything a wallet can claim, with one Claim all button.
  • Create: holder rewards for a token or an NFT collection, funded in one transaction, or a new NFT collection.
  • My campaigns: manage your campaigns and collections.

Every snapshot table is public on IPFS and its Merkle root is stored in the PlaceHub contract, so anyone can recompute it.
